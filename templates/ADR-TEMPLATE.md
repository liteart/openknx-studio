# ADR-NNN: Decision title

## Status

Proposed / Accepted / Rejected / Superseded. Decision owner and date. Related tasks and requirements.

## Context and evidence

Describe the actual problem and verified evidence. Separate user-confirmed constraints, measured facts, assumptions and missing inputs.

## Decision

State the chosen design, scope, affected repositories/contracts and exact invariants. A change to confirmed requirements needs explicit user/maintainer approval, not just an agent preference.

## Alternatives considered

Record realistic alternatives and why they were not chosen. Do not treat earlier superseded chat proposals as current requirements.

## Consequences and risks

List compatibility, license, security, performance, maintenance and migration impacts. State any new dependency or external prerequisite.

## Validation and rollout

Required tests/evidence, affected gates, migration/recovery plan and exact tasks to update. Define when the decision must be reconsidered.

## Approval

Named human approval, date and scope; otherwise this remains a proposal.
